# Farm-scale soil spectral library, Brazil (LF_BRA)

Farm-scale soil spectral library from two local farms in Brazil, 102 samples, VNIR 431–2153 nm.

The OSSL v1.3 release standardizes clay, organic carbon (ISO 10694), CEC, pH (CaCl₂, ISO 10390), and ion-exchange-resin-extractable P, K, Ca and Mg.

Original source: [Mendeley Data, doi:10.17632/88c5kvmgbf.1](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/88c5kvmgbf/1). Contact: Tiago Rodrigues Tavares (USP).

The earlier catalogue used the OSSL v1.3 representation. The current source checks and remaining limits are stated in verification_notes.


## Quality and caveats

**Record confidence: medium.** Mendeley v1 documents 102 soils, ID-linked fertility/VNIR/XRF/LIBS tables and CC BY 4.0. It is the same deposit family as the v2 tropical-soils entry. No numerical rows or OSSL-to-original equivalence were inspected; original organic matter and OSSL organic-carbon naming must not be treated as interchangeable measurements. The verification date is 2026-11-23 and covers current provider v1 documentation, not scientific accuracy or every resource component.


Review sources: [primary source 1](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/88c5kvmgbf/1).
