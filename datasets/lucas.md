# LUCAS datasets

The [LUCAS project](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/projects/lucas)

## Datasets
1. [LUCAS 2015 TOPSOIL data](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/lucas2015-topsoil-data)
    - soil properties: clay, silt and sand content, coarse fragments, pH (CaCl2 and H2O), organic carbon content, CaCO3, nitrogen, phosphorous, potassium, EC (Electrical conductivity
    - spectra: multispectral reflectance data for 21,859 samples
2. [Clay mineral inventory in soils of Europe based on LUCAS 2015 survey soil samples](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/clay-mineral-inventory-soils-europe-based-lucas-2015-survey-soil-samples-2)
3. [LUCAS 2022 TOPSOIL data](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/lucas-2022-topsoil-data)
    - 
4. [LUCAS 2018 TOPSOIL data](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/lucas-2018-topsoil-data)
5. [LUCAS 2009 TOPSOIL data](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/lucas-2009-topsoil-data)
    - soil properties: clay, silt and sand content, coarse fragments, pH, organic carbon content, CaCO3, nitrogen, phosphorous, potassium, cation exchane capacity 
    - spectra: multispectral absorbance data
    - 19,860 points
6. [LUCAS Heavy Metals](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/lucas-heavy-metals)
Heavy Metals from samples taken during the LUCAS 2009/2012 and 2018 campaigns
    - heavy metals (sheet 2009): As: Arsenic, Cd: Cadmium, Co: Cobalt, Cr: Cromium, Cu: Copper, Fe: Iron, Mg: Magnesium, Mn: Manganese, Ni: Nickel, Pb: Lead, Sb: Antimony, V: Vanadium, Zn: Zinc , Hg: Mercury
    - heavy metals (sheet 2018): As, Cd, Co, Cr, Cu, Ni, Pb, Sb, V, Zn, Hg
7. [LUCAS 2015 Topsoil data of Switzerland](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/lucas-2015-topsoil-data-switzerland)
    - soil properties: clay, silt and sand contents, organic carbon, nitrogen, potassium, phosphorus, calcium carbonate, pH and electrical conductivity
