# Farm-scale soil spectral library, Brazil (LF_BRA)

Farm-scale soil spectral library from two local farms in Brazil, 102 samples, VNIR 431–2153 nm.

The OSSL v1.3 release standardizes clay, organic carbon (ISO 10694), CEC, pH (CaCl₂, ISO 10390), and ion-exchange-resin-extractable P, K, Ca and Mg.

Original source: [Mendeley Data, doi:10.17632/88c5kvmgbf.1](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/88c5kvmgbf/1). Contact: Tiago Rodrigues Tavares (USP).

This record was assembled from the OSSL v1.3 catalogue; the Mendeley deposit was not reopened during the pass.
