# Datasets from papers

**Garrett, L. G., Sanderman, J., Palmer, D. J., Dean, F., Patel, S., Bridson, J. H., & Carlin, T. (2022). Mid-infrared spectroscopy for planted forest soil and foliage nutrition predictions, new zealand case study. Trees, Forests and People, 8, 100280**
- [paper link](10.1016/j.tfp.2022.100280): 
- [data link](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Soil_data_from_Pinus_radiata_forests_in_New_Zealand_FR380_trial_series/20506587/2)
- soil properties: soil pH, total carbon, total nitrogen, total phosphorus, available aluminium, available calcium, CEC (cation exchange capacity), base saturation, and the following elemental totals: aluminium, calcium, iron, potassium, magnesium, nickel, and zinc
- spectra: MIR

**Building a near-infrared (NIR) soil spectral dataset and predictive machine learning models using a handheld NIR spectrophotometer**
- [paper link](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2024.111229)
- [data link](https://zenodo.org/records/13122321)
- NIR spectra with 9 NeoSpectra
- 2,106 distinct mineral soil samples
- Sand, Silt, Clay, CEC, Exchangeable Ca, Exchangeable Mg, Exchangeable K, Exchangeable Na, CaCO3, OC, TN 

**Fohrafellner, Julia, Maximilian Lippl, Armin Bajraktarevic, Andreas Baumgarten, Heide Spiegel, Robert Körner, and Taru Sandén. 2026. “Austrian NIR Soil Spectral Library for Soil Health Assessments.” Earth System Science Data 18 (1): 219–29.**

- Description: An open NIR spectral library containing 2,129 soil samples from across Austria, with laboratory measurements of 14 chemical and physical soil-health properties.
- [paper link](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/18/219/2026/)
- [data link](https://zenodo.org/records/17941270)
- soil properties: soil organic carbon, total carbon, labile carbon, CaCO3, total nitrogen, plant-available phosphorus, pH, CEC, sand, silt and clay
- spectra: NIR absorbance spectra from 680 to 2500 nm at 1 nm resolution

**Mishra, Puneet, Theo Verkleij, and Ronald Klont. 2021. “Improved Prediction of Minced Pork Meat Chemical Properties with Near-Infrared Spectroscopy by a Fusion of Scatter-Correction Techniques.” Infrared Physics & Technology 113 (March): 103643.**

- Description: A public spectroscopy dataset containing 215 meat samples and their measured moisture, fat and protein measurements, can be used for testing regression models.
- [paper link](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1350449521000153)
- [data link](https://lib.stat.cmu.edu/datasets/tecator)
- food properties: moisture, fat and protein content
- spectra: NIR transmission absorbance spectra from 850 to 1050 nm, with 100 spectral channels

**Zhang, Qunjia, Lei Liu, and Jiacheng Mei. 2026. “A Multi-Scenario Proximal Hyperspectral Dataset of Clay-Type Lithium Deposits from China with Mineral Abundances and Geochemistry,” June.**

- Description: An open geological hyperspectral dataset containing powder, drill-core, hand-specimen and outcrop measurements from clay-type lithium deposits in southwest China.
- [paper link](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/407486299_A_multi-scenario_proximal_hyperspectral_dataset_of_clay-type_lithium_deposits_from_China_with_mineral_abundances_and_geochemistry)
- [data link](https://zenodo.org/records/19142250)
- geological properties: lithium and other geochemical measurements, quantitative X-ray diffraction results and mineral abundances
- spectra: SWIR hyperspectral reflectance or relative-reflectance data from approximately 960 to 2500 nm
