# Martinique NIR soil spectral library (MTQ)

National NIR soil spectral library from rural areas of Martinique, 98 profiles and 516 tabular observations (provider metadata); the previously reported NIR range is 1100–2498 nm and files remain uninspected.

The OSSL v1.3 release standardizes organic carbon (ISO 10694), total nitrogen (ISO 13878), clay, sand and silt (ISO 11277), bulk density, and coarse fragments (ISO 11464).

Original source: [IRD Dataverse, doi:10.23708/C2TV6W](https://dataverse.ird.fr/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.23708/C2TV6W). Contact: Aurélie Cambou (IRD).

The earlier catalogue used the OSSL v1.3 representation. The current source checks and remaining limits are stated in verification_notes.


## Quality and caveats

**Record confidence: medium.** The institutional page identifies 98 profiles and 516 tabular observations, describes SOC/texture/NIR content and explicitly requires CC BY-NC 4.0. The prior CC BY licence and description of 516 independent samples were corrected. Numerical spectra, populated target counts and ID alignment were not inspected. The verification date is 2026-11-23 and covers current institutional Dataverse documentation and terms, not scientific accuracy or every resource component.
