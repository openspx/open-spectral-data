# National Virtual Core Library (NVCL)

The NVCL is the world's largest online drill core mineralogical database, with over 1.6 million meters of drill core data  freely accessible to users anywhere in the world. 

The NVCL datasets are available via the [AuScope Discovery Portal](https://portal.auscope.org.au/).
