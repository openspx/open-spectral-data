# Martinique NIR soil spectral library (MTQ)

National NIR soil spectral library from rural areas of Martinique, 516 samples, 1100–2498 nm.

The OSSL v1.3 release standardizes organic carbon (ISO 10694), total nitrogen (ISO 13878), clay, sand and silt (ISO 11277), bulk density, and coarse fragments (ISO 11464).

Original source: [IRD Dataverse, doi:10.23708/C2TV6W](https://dataverse.ird.fr/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.23708/C2TV6W). Contact: Aurélie Cambou (IRD).

This record was assembled from the OSSL v1.3 catalogue; the Dataverse deposit was not reopened during the pass.
