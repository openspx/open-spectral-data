# Thailand agricultural soil homemade-NIR dataset

## Summary

Thailand agricultural soil homemade-NIR dataset. Experimental numerical measurements are documented; the checks below establish the reviewed scope.

## Provenance and citation

[Author/provider deposit](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/yt78nwnhbd/1). [Accompanying research/data paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2025.111840). 

## Acquisition

Homemade spectrometer using DLP NIRscan Nano. Stored signal: absorbance; 'other' means the stored signal definition or non-optical intensity requires clarification.

## Content and reference variables

Reported 190 topsoils (0–20 cm) from vegetable farms, orchards and field crops in northern Thailand. The paper identifies a workbook containing averaged raw/preprocessed spectra together with chemical references.

Soil organic matter; Total carbon. Target origins and unresolved units are specified above.
